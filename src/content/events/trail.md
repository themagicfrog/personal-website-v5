From July 11, 2024 to July 20, 2024, I went on a life-changing trip on the Pacific Crest Trail, a 2650 mile long trail from Mexico to Canada. Over the course of a week, I hiked 30 miles alongside 29 teenage hackers from 9 different countries and 15 states! This event was organized by Hack Club, community of over 34,000 teenage makers and creators! This adventure followed the month long PCB hackathon, where people worked together to design printed circuit boards to use on the hike, like walkie talkies and flashlights. The Trail was an adventure of a lifetime.
