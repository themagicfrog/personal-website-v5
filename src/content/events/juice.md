The Juice popup cafe was a two month online game jam followed by an in-person popup hacker cafe in Shanghai, China. Over 90 teenagers from 23 countries flew to Shanghai, China to present their games in our popup cafe and juice a bunch of fruits to give to the locals as they tried their game. I helped organize this event as the lead designer. I drew all the assets on the website, which helped Juice receive 2000 signups. For this event, I made the Signal Lost game. Juice was an amazing experience!
